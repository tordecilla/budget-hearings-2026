At a glance

  • Athlete support and federation accountability: Senators Christopher Lawrence T. Go, Joel Villanueva and Joseph Victor "JV" G. Ejercito raised athlete welfare, delayed training allowances and concerns about politics and leadership in the Philippine National Volleyball Federation (PNVF) and national sports associations (NSAs). Philippine Sports Commission (PSC) Chairman John Patrick C. Gregorio distinguished travel support from training allowances and said the PSC funds and oversees programs while NSAs manage athletes. Senators pressed for timely support and greater athlete representation in sports bodies.
  • Sports-association financial assistance: Ejercito raised Commission on Audit (COA) findings about unliquidated financial assistance to the PNVF and the Gymnastics Association of the Philippines. COA Supervising Auditor Jeorge Roman G. Lozano gave records and an effective balance on different accounting bases; Gregorio said associations that hosted international events still needed to liquidate advances before the PSC could keep funding them. The hearing did not reconcile the different balances.
  • Facilities and regional access: Gregorio described repairs to abandoned sites, proposed and ongoing sports facilities, and plans to complete regional training centers so athletes could train closer to home. Senators asked how budget reductions affected those priorities and encouraged local and private partners to help fund facilities.
  • Professional-athlete eligibility and support: Villanueva asked which athletes qualified for PSC assistance and whether support ended after major competitions. Gregorio cited the charter's amateur-athlete focus as a constraint. PSC OIC Executive Director Attorney Guillermo B. Iroy Jr. later clarified that professional players representing the country may receive event-related support through their NSA, including endorsed training camps, while PSC cannot fund club representation.
  • Meals and athlete welfare review: National jiu-jitsu athlete Kimberly Anne Custodio asked whether meal service replaced a monthly allowance and described an inadequate meal portion and an angry staff response. Gregorio explained the meal-or-allowance option for athletes training away from PSC facilities, apologized for the interaction and promised an immediate review with the nutritionist.
  • Sports-fund remittances and public oversight: Go called for the Philippine Amusement and Gaming Corporation (PAGCOR) to comply with its statutory sports-development remittance to the PSC. Gregorio said PAGCOR had resumed payments and sought continued committee support for regional centers; Games and Amusements Board (GAB) Chairperson Attorney Francisco J. Rivera asked the committee to support the board's proposed allocation.
  • Professional-sports regulation: Rivera described licensing, enforcement, insurance and due-process responsibilities. Senators pressed for action on game-fixing and asked how professional athletes were defined and supported when representing the country.
  • Submission to plenary: Go said the committee was submitting the PSC and GAB budgets to plenary after considering the hearing discussion, then suspended the hearing.

Games and Amusements Board presentation
