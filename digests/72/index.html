At a glance

  • Opening priorities and budget presentation: Subcommittee G chair Senator Francis Kiko Pangilinan opened the hearing by stressing restoration, farmer access to water, project quality and public transparency. Senator Imee R. Marcos questioned the National Irrigation Administration (NIA) on its requested budget and performance. NIA Administrator Eduardo Eddie G. Guillen presented the agency's irrigation plans, digital project-tracking platform and funding priorities.
  • Oversight and project accountability: Marcos and Pangilinan pressed NIA and the Commission on Audit (COA) on non-operational systems, delayed or terminated contracts, procurement findings and asset records. The agency and auditors undertook to provide updated project-level and implementation information. NIA and the Department of Budget and Management (DBM) also discussed how a validated project list could support early procurement while linking released funds to approved projects; DBM was asked to submit its proposed safeguard in writing.
  • Resilience, farmer support and water storage: The discussion covered drought preparation, solar irrigation, restoration, dam operations, flood-mitigation and storage proposals, and farmer sales and cooperative development. Senators asked for clearer dam-release protocols, project costs and status, and evidence of irrigation and farmer benefits.
  • Finance Committee priorities and disposition: Senate Committee on Finance chair Senator Joseph Victor "JV" G. Ejercito emphasized measurable irrigation results and long-term water storage, and raised project duration and funding status. Pangilinan closed by deeming the NIA budget submitted for plenary consideration subject to requested submissions and a separate preparatory meeting; this was not approval of the appropriation.
