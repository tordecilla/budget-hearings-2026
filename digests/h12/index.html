At a glance

  • Resources for public audit: COA entered the FY 2027 briefing with no capital outlay in the NEP and a larger original proposal. Chairperson Gamaliel A. Cordoba and officials faced questions about auditor shortages; members sought stronger preventive audit and digital capacity.
  • Preventing and investigating irregularities: Members pressed for pre-audit with geotagging, follow-through on flood-control findings and tighter confidential-fund rules. COA described fraud and performance audit work, offered further reports and outlined proposed rule changes requiring inter-agency coordination. Audit notices were discussed as findings subject to process, not proven losses or convictions.
  • Cash balances versus cash access: Rep. Roberto Nazal challenged whether earmarked and off-budget balances prove money is available and warned against project nonimplementation being used to manufacture savings. Officials distinguished audit of collections and spending from executive cash-management decisions. COA undertook to examine disputed DPWH cases and consider a monitoring protocol with DBM and Treasury.
  • Reports Congress can use: Reps. Iris Marie Demesa Montes and Mikaela Angela B. Suansing sought timely, understandable audit reports for budget scrutiny. COA distinguished transmission to agencies and Congress from later website publication. Cordoba committed to resend reports the next morning and check the outstanding NHA and Judiciary submissions.
  • Requests and outcome: Suansing requested a detailed, prioritized amendment list that week for subcommittee review; Cordoba agreed. She cautioned that fiscal space would limit funding and did not promise a higher budget. The COA briefing was terminated and committee proceedings suspended; no final augmentation amount was approved in the closing motions.
