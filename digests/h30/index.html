At a glance

  • Proposed investments: Department of Transportation (DOTr)'s proposed investments covered rail, road, aviation and maritime projects, but members questioned which projects were funded, partly funded or left without proposed allocations.
  • Release and utilization: Members and officials discussed how foreign-loan proceeds, counterpart funding, right-of-way acquisition and unprogrammed appropriations affect project release, utilization, delays and financing costs.
  • Commuter programs: Members sought stronger commuter support and challenged proposed cuts to EDSA Bus Carousel service contracting and fuel subsidies; DOTr supported restoration, with no committee funding decision in that exchange.
  • Regional projects and promised reports: Regional requests included rail connectivity, right-of-way, ports, roads and airport improvements. Some remained unfunded or conditional on inspection, while agencies promised project lists, timelines, delay reports and alignment documents.
  • Budget augmentation proposal: A proposed lump-sum augmentation for unfunded projects and studies remained pending without a vote; other increase requests and expressions of support also remained distinct from adopted appropriations.
  • Maritime safety and suspension: Members questioned Maritime Industry Authority (MARINA) on inspection staffing and technical-capacity needs. The hearing then recessed for lunch; the recess did not approve or conclude the budget deliberation.

Department of Transportation (DOTr) budget

DOTr 2027 NEP allocation and budget composition: For fiscal year 2027, the DOTr NEP allocation was ₱300.96 billion, as presented by DOTr Assistant Secretary Cecilia E. Bien. That was ₱103 billion (52.52%) above its FY2026 NEP figure of ₱197.3 billion; against the FY2026 GAA amount of ₱137.846 billion, the FY2027 NEP was ₱163.114 billion (118%) higher. These figures are stated as components of the allocation; the transcript total in that breakdown is garbled. The presentation did not give a distinct total for DOTr's original agency-wide submission to Department of Budget and Management (DBM); the later ₱624 billion figure is specifically its proposed projects, not an established total agency request. For the Office of the Secretary, Central Office alone, FY2025 obligation and disbursement rates were 90.45% and 57.45%; as of July 2026 they were 82.40% and 51.73%. Across DOTr and attached agencies, FY2025 rates were 89.35% and 56.94%, and as of July 2026 they were 74.10% and 56.18%.
