Official House of Representatives livestream

At a glance

  • Budget and priorities: Acting Secretary Juan Miguel Cuna presented a roughly P28-billion DENR proposal covering forest restoration, land patents, mineral development, water services and ecosystem protection. Members pressed DENR, MGB, EMB, BMB, LWUA, NWRB and LLDA on local delivery and funding gaps.
  • Flooding and water: Members called for coordinated river-basin planning, stronger watershed and mangrove funding, hazard-informed infrastructure and assessment of Bulacan Airport's effects on Pampanga. DENR described plans still under review and assessment, acknowledged resource limits and promised follow-up studies and coordination.
  • Mining and land: Disputed permits, consultation, quarrying, forestland titles and rehabilitation dominated several rounds. DENR promised investigations and records, a Siruma reversion filing target, and rapid Homonhon assessment results before plenary; proposed bans and cancellations were not generally announced as completed actions.
  • Waste and public services: The exchanges covered solid-waste implementation, future solar and battery waste, imported e-waste, Manila Bay enforcement, threatened trees, lake-shore displacement and strained water districts. Several requests ended in promised reports, referrals or unresolved questions rather than final decisions.
  • Committee outcome: The chair qualified the roughly P556-million augmentation motion by available fiscal space. Suansing requested a formal, prioritized DENR submission for the Budget Amendments Review Subcommittee. The committee terminated the DENR briefing and suspended proceedings until August 27 at 9 a.m.; it did not enact the budget.

Agency presentation

P28.04-billion proposal and natural-capital priorities: Acting Secretary Juan Miguel Cuna sought P28.04 billion for DENR, its 17 regional offices, bureaus and attached agencies. With a mandate covering 15.8 million hectares of classified forestlands and over 36,000 kilometers of coastline, he framed the priorities around community impact despite limited fiscal space. He proposed shifting the National Greening Program toward forest-landscape restoration and community enterprises, issuing up to 43,000 land patents in 2027, and pursuing a 2% mining contribution to GDP by 2028 through small-scale mining support, critical-mineral mapping and regulatory enforcement.

Water, waste and ecosystem resilience: Cuna proposed P124 million in assistance to local water districts for isolated and unserved barangays. He reported that extended producer responsibility had mobilized over 1,000 enterprises and diverted 244 million kilograms of packaging waste, alongside continued work on Manila Bay, the Pasig River and Laguna Lake. Cuna reported an operating 24-hour geohazards center, interventions targeting 18 high-exposure provinces, and stronger management of 121 legislated protected areas and marine networks. DENR was building technical capacity and staffing to establish site-specific ecosystem accounts under PENCAS for planning and budgets.
