At a glance

  • Commission on Audit (COA) and audit capacity: Members examined COA’s ability to expand audits, modernize its systems, retain specialists and extend audit coverage.
  • Commission on Elections (COMELEC) and election readiness: COMELEC presented election preparations; members questioned voting-machine procurement, online voting education and overseas voter access.
  • Anti-Red Tape Authority (ARTA) and public services: The ARTA exchanges covered regulatory compliance, regional capacity, complaint handling, digital systems and the effects of reduced operating support.
  • Anti-Money Laundering Council (AMLC), safeguards and recovery: AMLC interpellations covered risk-proportionate safeguards for nonprofit organizations, confidentiality, online sexual abuse and exploitation of children reporting, freeze orders, forfeitures, and whether frozen assets had actually been recovered.
  • National Intelligence Coordinating Agency (NICA): NICA’s sponsor briefly presented the proposed budget and offered to answer questions; no substantive interpellation followed.
  • National Security Council (NSC), policy and accountability: Members questioned the NSC journal’s peer review, authorship and treatment of civic activity; they raised rights, privacy and human-security concerns and debated alliances, defense posture, staffing and confidential spending.
  • National Amnesty Commission (NAC): Members questioned slow amnesty processing, the gap between applications and presidential grants, and administrative constraints; the sponsor outlined remedies without a firm completion timetable.
  • Office of the Presidential Adviser on Peace, Reconciliation and Unity (OPAPRU): OPAPRU interpellations covered PAMANA implementation and audits, normalization, education assistance, transitional justice, and women’s participation and security.
