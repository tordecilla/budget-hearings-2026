At a glance

  • Justice and enforcement capacity: Subcommittee G chair Senator Francis Kiko Pangilinan and Senators Panfilo Lacson and Joseph Victor "JV" G. Ejercito pressed Secretary Fredderick A. Vida and officials of the Department of Justice (DOJ) and its attached agencies on whether resources could support effective prosecutions, investigations, victim protection and fair treatment in custody. They highlighted staffing and equipment constraints at the National Bureau of Investigation (NBI), online child-protection funding, and agricultural-smuggling case-building. DOJ described its case-disposition calculation and agreed to add an explanatory notation and provide more disaggregated case information.
  • Revenue, restoration and modernization: Members sought clearer accounting for DOJ-family remittances and returns, special accounts, confidential-fund levels, witness protection and agency operating needs. The Bureau of Immigration (BI) explained the express-lane split, while the NBI requested higher agent hazard pay and Lacson identified its statutory basis. Members also examined immigration-system phases and the proposed Civil Aviation and Immigration Security Services partnership; BI said the proposal remained under review, with no final decision. The restoration and augmentation proposals discussed across the hearing were not recorded as approved appropriations.
  • Corrections, food systems and plenary conditions: DOJ and Bureau of Corrections (BuCor) officials discussed a stepwise transfer of the Bureau of Jail Management and Penology (BJMP) toward DOJ, with a technical working group with the Department of the Interior and Local Government (DILG) preparing a draft executive order and provincial-jail responsibilities identified as a legal issue. Senators also examined direct purchasing from farmers and fisherfolk, food procurement and agricultural-smuggling enforcement. DOJ agreed to provide input at a separate meeting before plenary. Pangilinan said he would endeavor to endorse restoration requests and deemed the budget submitted for plenary consideration subject to the requested information and separate meeting; this was not an appropriation approval.
Reading note

Budget amounts are separated by status: agency submission, NEP recommendation, prior-year approved budget, and later restoration or augmentation requests. Hearing statements, estimates and unresolved scope differences remain attributed to the speakers. A request, expression of support or conditional plenary submission does not itself approve an appropriation.
