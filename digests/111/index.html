At a glance

  • Defense priorities: DND Secretary Gilberto C. Teodoro Jr. presented the Department of National Defense (DND) and Armed Forces of the Philippines (AFP) priorities, linking territorial defense with modernization, cyber preparedness, logistics and disaster response.
  • Budget and delivery questions: Senators examined the request and National Expenditure Program (NEP) comparison, appropriation execution, modernization and infrastructure shortfalls, and coordination over defense-related public works projects.
  • Agency programs and capacity: Discussion covered disaster-response coordination, veterans' health and hospice access, domestic munitions production, and the staffing and supply constraints affecting program delivery.
  • Security relationships and policy: Members discussed visiting-forces arrangements, joint exercises and interoperability, deterrence, local defense production, and Teodoro's characterization of propaganda and political activity.
  • Closing and disposition: Committee chair Senator Joseph Victor "JV" G. Ejercito distinguished the Chinese people from government actions, affirmed mutual respect and Philippine sovereignty, and stated that the DND budget would proceed to plenary consideration subject to discussion and possible adjustment.
