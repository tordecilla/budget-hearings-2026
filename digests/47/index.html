At a glance

  • Budget changes and restoration requests: Members sought restoration of Philippine Health Insurance Corporation (PhilHealth) subsidies, hospital operating funds, facility funding and other health services. Department of Health (DOH) was asked to justify cuts and submit supporting records; funding increases were raised for consideration.
  • Hospital access, facilities and staffing: Members raised regional access gaps, delayed or incomplete projects, hospital-bed expansions and shortages of staff. DOH described facility-planning criteria and follow-up work on project inventories, site suitability and staffing information.
  • Patient assistance and hospital billing: Questions covered MAIFIP access, zero-balance billing, professional fees, charity care and patient complaints. Members sought clearer rules, oversight and reports on how assistance and hospital services reach patients.
  • Medicines, procurement and prevention: The discussion covered medicine purchasing and distribution, TB and vaccine procurement, expiring stocks, local production and preventive health programs. Members pressed agencies on delivery and implementation. Food and Drug Administration (FDA) funding, staffing, application backlogs and digital modernization were also examined.
  • PhilHealth services and follow-up: Members raised YAKAP and GAMOT access, ambulance benefits, provider accountability and benefit design. The committee and agency officials identified information and policy follow-up on health programs and public spending.
