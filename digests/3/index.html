PAGCOR presentation

Income performance, gaming decline, and the 2027 outlook: PAGCOR presented PHP 106.03 billion in total income for 2025, an estimated PHP 86.95 billion for 2026, and projected 2027 income of PHP 88.33 billion with PHP 5.45 billion in net income. It reported PHP 95.14 billion in 2025 gaming-operations income, down PHP 2.38 billion from 2024, and attributed the decline to the POGO closure, weaker PAGCOR-casino and licensee revenue, and the delinking of e-wallets from online gaming platforms. The 2026 and 2027 amounts were estimates and projections, not reported final results.

00:36:21 · asr-000120

Taxes, government shares, dividends, and universal-health funding: The presentation described PAGCOR's statutory distribution structure, including the five-percent franchise tax, the National Government share, host-city and socio-civic allocations, and mandated contributions. It reported PHP 66.95 billion in 2025 nation-building contributions, PHP 8.31 billion in BIR tax remittances, PHP 5.67 billion in declared dividends, and about PHP 22.56 billion as the 2025 allocation associated with universal health care. For the first half of 2026, it reported PHP 43.32 billion in total income and PHP 30.16 billion for nation-building programs.

School buildings, medical transport, disaster aid, and hospital equipment: PAGCOR presented a PHP 7.5-billion school-building commitment covering 125 three-storey, 12-classroom buildings—about 1,500 classrooms over two years—with DepEd recommendations and recipient-LGU completion capacity guiding site selection. It also reported close to 250 donated patient-transport vehicles, PHP 300 million spent on food and non-food disaster packs in 2025 and the first half of 2026, and PHP 360 million in PAGCOR medical equipment for the Jose B. Lingad Children's Medical Center, alongside PHP 1 billion from Bloomberry Cultural Foundation for construction and development.

Lower gaming income and PAGCOR's recovery outlook: Suansing asked why the stated 2026 total-income projection of about PHP 86.95 billion was 18 percent below the PHP 106-billion 2025 actual income. PAGCOR Chairman Alejandro Tengco attributed the decline to the delinking of e-wallet payment applications from online gaming, which he said was followed by about a 40-percent fall in gaming activity, and to the Middle East crisis affecting land-based and online markets. Nation-building contributions, PSC payments, and socio-civic spending: Suansing noted that, despite a roughly PHP 20-billion income reduction, PAGCOR's contributions to nation building would fall only PHP 5.5 billion, or 8.3 percent, from the PHP 66.9-billion 2025 level; she also identified reported socio-civic projects rising from PHP 12.4 billion in 2024 to PHP 12.8 billion in 2025 and PHP 13.99 billion in 2026.
