At a glance

  • Agency budget review: The committee reviewed the Department of Human Settlements and Urban Development (DHSUD), Human Settlements Adjudication Commission (HSAC), National Housing Authority (NHA), Social Housing Finance Corporation (SHFC), National Home Mortgage Finance Corporation (NHMFC), and Home Development Mutual Fund (Pag-IBIG Fund), distinguishing proposed appropriations, original submissions and corporate financing.
  • Eligibility and occupancy: Senator Raffy T. Tulfo pressed the NHA on allegations of favoritism in beneficiary lists, independent eligibility checks, resale of housing rights, warnings to buyers, and monitoring after families move in. NHA General Manager Joeben A. Tai described cancellation and reassignment procedures and committed to stronger vetting and monitoring.
  • Delivery and affordability: Members raised water access, flooding, emergency shelter, housing payment terms, and the reach of the expanded Pambansang Pabahay para sa Pilipino (4PH) program and community-mortgage options. Agency replies described ongoing programs and distinctions in how beneficiaries qualify and pay.
  • Implementation and oversight: Senators Christopher Lawrence T. Go and Joel Villanueva questioned utilization, subsidy timing, licensing, staffing, and housing targets. DHSUD Secretary Jose Ramon P. Aliling and Senior Undersecretary Henry L. Yap explained reported measures and processes, while some requested targets and follow-up information remained outstanding.
  • Project funding follow-up: Majority Leader Senator Juan Miguel "Migz" F. Zubiri raised delayed cash for Bukidnon resettlement projects and a separate Panantukan request. The Department of Budget and Management (DBM) and NHA gave conflicting accounts of available cash; Aliling committed to a formal report and Tai said NHA would fully fund the projects.
  • Committee disposition: Hearing chair Senator Joseph Victor "JV" G. Ejercito identified priority housing requests and said the committee would try to help within available fiscal space. He submitted the DHSUD and attached-agency budgets for plenary consideration, subject to further discussion and requested documents.
