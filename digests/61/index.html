  • The proposed budget and agency requests: Presiding Rep. JC Rahman A. Nava, M.D. and Committee Chair Rep. Mikaela Angela B.   • Requests, follow-ups and closing: Members expressed support for several increases, and officials undertook to provide information or add needs to later submissions. Those requests and commitments were not appropriation approvals. Presiding Rep. Nava approved procedural motions to terminate this hearing and suspend proceedings without prejudice to a concurrent hearing elsewhere.

Agency budget summary

FY2027 DSWD and connected-agency NEP allocations: For FY2027, the National Expenditure Program (NEP) proposes PHP 241.2 billion for the Department of Social Welfare and Development (DSWD) and the attached and supervised agencies included in its presentation. The DSWD Office of the Secretary accounts for PHP 235.7 billion. Attached-agency amounts are PHP 136.3 million for the Council for the Welfare of Children (CWC), PHP 91.6 million for the National Council on Disability Affairs (NCDA), PHP 126.3 million for the Juvenile Justice and Welfare Council (JJWC), and PHP 811.8 million for the National Authority for Child Care (NACC). Supervised-agency amounts are PHP 3.8 billion for the National Commission of Senior Citizens (NCSC), PHP 359.9 million for the National Anti-Poverty Commission (NAPC), and PHP 233.4 million for the Presidential Commission for the Urban Poor (PCUP). The presentation says FY2027 NEP levels for DSWD and the included agencies are generally below FY2026 General Appropriations Act (GAA) levels, except for personnel-service increases at NACC and NCSC associated with new or filled positions. It provides no comparable numeric FY2026 GAA total for this agency set. Executive Order 123 placed NAPC and PCUP under the Office of the President for administrative supervision, although their FY2027 figures remain in the DSWD presentation.
