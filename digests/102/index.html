Rep. Antonio L. Tinio: Rep. Antonio L. Tinio framed the 2027 budget debate around who benefits, arguing that the national budget sustains privatization, deregulation and liberalization. He said these policies concentrated essential services and infrastructure in a few conglomerates and brought high costs, while small businesses, low-income professionals and workers faced expensive electricity and credit, import competition, low pay or overtaxation. He linked unemployment and underemployment to neglected agriculture and the lack of domestic industry, arguing that service-sector jobs alone cannot employ the country’s people. In his view, a growing safety net of ayuda and politicized social services reflects economic failure rather than broad-based prosperity.

Tinio cited Department of Public Works and Highways (DPWH) capital-outlay figures to argue that flood control grew from 20% in 2016 to about ₱357 billion, or 36.6% of DPWH capital outlay, by 2024; he added that more than ₱1 out of every ₱3 spent on public works under the administration went to flood control.
