At a glance

  • FY 2027 food-security and irrigation case: DA presented food security, farmer and fisherfolk livelihoods, productivity, logistics, resilience and accountability as the case for its FY 2027 request. NIA paired its development, repair and climate-adaptation agenda with a national irrigation coverage gap, but said the NEP allocation was far below its proposed budget.
  • Irrigation funding, selection and flexibility: Across multiple regional rounds, members challenged low or zero allocations, delayed repairs and the basis for project priorities. NIA repeatedly cited limited fiscal space and ongoing Tier 1 commitments, while asking to return to a program-based appropriation structure that could redirect funds from delayed projects to validated, implementation-ready work with oversight.
  • Farm roads, post-harvest systems and disclosure: Members described a large farm-to-market-road backlog, missing access roads, delayed releases, high per-kilometre costs and questions about project coordinates and unusually large projects. DA linked roads, cold storage, processing, fish ports and logistics to lower losses and better market access, and undertook to provide project reports and coordinate on identified entries.
  • Livestock, biosecurity and import dependence: The discussion connected ASF, delayed indemnification, vaccine access, animal repopulation, dairy and carabao distribution, pest response and import controls to farmgate prices and domestic supply. DA officials described proposed tariff and animal-industry measures, while several promised animal deliveries and local interventions remained conditional, delayed or subject to further follow-up.
  • Regional commodity programs and agency follow-through: Members sought support for abaca, coconut, palm oil, banana, sugar, high-value crops, fisheries and aquaculture, often pairing local project requests with questions about agency execution. Officials expressed support for selected proposals, studies, coordination and future requests, but many funding levels, legislation, procurement actions and local allocations remained unresolved. The frozen hearing ends with a short health-break suspension.

Food security and producer livelihoods as the budget test: DA Secretary Francisco P. Agricultural conditions and production bottlenecks: Undersecretary Asis G. Perez reported 3.1% agriculture, forestry and fisheries gross-value-added growth in 2025 after a 1.5% contraction in 2024, while the sector’s share of the economy was 7.9%.
