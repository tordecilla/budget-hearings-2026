At a glance

  • Budget request: The FY 2027 DBM recommendation presented for the Office of the Ombudsman was ₱7.232934 billion, with the Office also seeking additional resources and special provisions.
  • Committee process: The chair asked the Office to submit a written, prioritized list of additional requests for possible BAR C consideration; Remulla said the Office would comply as soon as possible.
  • Case performance: The hearing examined case performance and capacity, including a reported 65.52% Sandiganbayan conviction rate as of June 30, 2026, staffing, digital forensics, and regional access.
  • Accountability: Members pressed for evidence-based accountability in flood-control and other corruption matters; the Office described ongoing review and monitoring, not completed findings against named people.
  • Institutional safeguards: Support for proposed Ombudsman marshals, special provisions, and other institutional measures was voiced, but no proposal was adopted or funded in the briefing.
  • Outcome: The Ombudsman briefing was terminated on a moved-and-seconded motion, and a 3 p.m. Commission on Audit budget briefing was announced.
