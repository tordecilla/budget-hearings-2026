At a glance

  • Funding and memorialization: Members pressed to restore Commission on Human Rights (CHR) capacity and sought a prioritized funding list. The Human Rights Violations Victims Memorial Commission (HRVVMC) explained the status and release process for memorialization trust funds.
  • Investigation and oversight: Members questioned drug-war reinvestigations, older case outcomes, detention monitoring and red-tagging. The commission promised written updates and year-end determinations for the cases ready for resolution.
  • Protection and education: The hearing covered gender-based complaints, community education, digital harms and threats to defenders. Members asked for stronger responses and more specific reports on rights risks.
Reading note

Figures distinguish the CHR's original FY2027 submission from the NEP allocation and the separate FY2026 trust-interest provision. Restoration proposals, report commitments and committee outcomes retain their actual status.
