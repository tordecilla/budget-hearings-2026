Department of Public Works and Highways (DPWH): Committee on Appropriations vice chair Representative Romeo S. Momo Sr. sponsored the Department of Public Works and Highways (DPWH) proposal. He identified ₱199.58 billion for Organizational Outcome 1 (national roads, bridges and related infrastructure), ₱83.84 billion for Organizational Outcome 2 (flood control, seawalls and related infrastructure), and ₱117.74 billion for foreign-assisted projects covering roads, bridges, flood mitigation and the national building program.

Momo said the flood-control program would prioritize finishing incomplete structures, closing gaps and repairing pumping stations, drainage and floodgates according to river-basin and drainage master plans, while continuing foreign-assisted work such as the Pasig–Marikina Channel Improvement. As of September 23, 2026, he reported nine contractors blacklisted and more than 80 administrative cases being pursued.

Momo said Luzon had received an average 56% of allocations over the prior decade, while its share in 2027 would fall to 52.62% as the Visayas and Mindanao together rose to about 47%. He characterized the proposed budget as shifting emphasis from building more to building better and asked the House to support it.
