At a glance

  • Markets, branding and workforce: Members discussed domestic and international market priorities, tourism competitiveness, destination branding, transparency and trust, and training and certification for tourism workers. The proposed BINI and SB19 campaign collaboration remained under discussion, with a September deadline mentioned. Members also raised destination readiness and matching investment to infrastructure and market capacity.
  • Access and destination infrastructure: Members raised airfare and fuel pressures, constrained island air capacity and airport operating hours, and asked about coordination with the Department of Transportation (DOTr) and the Civil Aeronautics Board (CAB). They also discussed tourism roads and facilities, regional infrastructure planning, and projects or access concerns in Northern Mindanao, Negros Occidental, Dinagat, Catanduanes and Western Visayas. TIEZA’s role in tourism facilities was distinguished from road construction.
  • Resilience, safety and local participation: Members asked how disaster-affected tourism workers and small businesses would be assessed and connected with Department of Social Welfare and Development (DSWD) and local-government food or cash assistance. The discussion also covered environmental safeguards and community participation, women’s and children’s safety and referral services, relocation-related facilities in Intramuros, and support for local businesses and tourism enterprises.
  • Committee disposition: Rep. Julienne "Jam" L. Baronda moved to terminate the FY2027 Department of Tourism (DOT) budget hearing, including its attached agencies and corporations. Rep. Howard A. Guintu seconded, and presiding Rep. Marie Bernadette G. Escudero approved without objection; a separate motion to suspend was also approved. Tourism Secretary Dita Angara-Mathay thanked the committee at her first DOT budget hearing and said the requests articulated that day would be fulfilled by the next hearing.
