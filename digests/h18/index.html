Agency presentation

A smaller communications budget: FY 2027 PCO-family NEP: PHP 2.621 billion including corporate subsidies, against PHP 2.768 billion in the FY 2026 NEP for the same family. Gomez separately gave about PHP 2.48 billion for the FY 2027 department budget excluding GOCC subsidies. Gomez described an expanding demand for accurate, accessible government information despite reduced resources. The agency allocations he outlined included ₱784 million for PCO proper, ₱697 million for PIA, ₱499 million for the Bureau of Broadcast Services, ₱240 million for RTVM, ₱157 million for the News and Information Bureau, and ₱80 million for the Bureau of Communication Services. PTV would receive ₱138 million for revitalization and digitalization; IBC would receive no subsidy because it remained classified for privatization. APO Production Unit would operate from corporate earnings.

Public information, media literacy and digital production: PCO's program combined presidential coverage and coordinated agency messaging with freedom of information, fact-checking workshops, regional and provincial communication, public radio, and government print and audiovisual production. Its priorities included media literacy, an anti-fake-news reporting desk with referrals to appropriate agencies, digital platforms and audience analytics, and additional Bagong Pilipinas Studios. Gomez also proposed energy-saving equipment and transport measures. He framed these activities around simpler internal processes, decisions informed by data, measurable accountability, and stronger staff capabilities.

Budget proposal and requests

FY 2027 PCO NEP and original request: The FY2027 NEP allocation for PCO and its attached agencies and corporations was PHP 2.621 billion, as presiding Rep. Alexandria "Queenie" Gonzales stated, down from PHP 2.768 billion in the FY 2026 NEP. PCO Secretary David M. Gomez separately put the 2027 department budget excluding GOCC subsidies at about PHP 2.48 billion. PCO proper was allocated PHP 784 million, with no capital outlay; Gomez said DBM omitted proposed Tier 2 projects and reduced its operating and capital requests. Original requests discussed in the hearing concern individual offices and projects; no PCO-family aggregate submission is given.

Rep. Roberto Nazal

Why PIA increased while other offices were cut: Nazal asked why the department's proposed allocation fell while PIA's increased. Gomez attributed part of PCO proper's reduction to the end of the Philippines' ASEAN hosting year, and said the major reason for PIA's increase was retrofitting its deteriorated, 44-year-old building. Protect employees and preserve Congress TV through privatization: Co-Pilar urged a transitional IBC budget to preserve operations and the station's value pending privatization. Closing decisions

PCO hearing terminated; committee proceedings suspended: On Rep. Julienne "Jam" L. Baronda's motion, seconded by Yamsuan, the committee terminated the hearing on the proposed FY2027 PCO budget and its attached agencies and corporations without objection.
