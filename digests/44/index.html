At a glance

  • One test across four agencies: The committee considered FY 2027 NEP allocations and agency proposals for MTRCB, OMB, FDCP and PHILRACOM. MTRCB paired its appropriation with expected collections; FDCP and PHILRACOM sought additional funding. Senator Erwin Tulfo asked how these resources would benefit workers, consumers, artists and businesses.
  • MTRCB and the limits of current online oversight: MTRCB combined its agency appropriation with separate retirement premiums and collections in a broader operating plan. It reported classification, compliance, revenue and public-education activity. Senator Erwin Tulfo asked about online platforms, games and social media; officials said expanded oversight would require legislation and platform cooperation. Access to the sinking fund remained subject to DBM approval.
  • OMB between digital piracy and an unresolved transition: OMB described copyright and licensing duties and growing digital piracy under its current legal mandate. DBM provided Tier 1 funding while the Government Optimization Plan and possible staff transfers remained pending. Tulfo said employees should be transferred rather than dismissed.
  • FDCP balancing production support and film preservation: FDCP described a lower NEP allocation and additional requests for grants, location incentives, film preservation and facilities. Tulfo asked it to prioritize requests for plenary discussion. Release of earlier unreleased balances depended on special budget requests and supporting documents.
  • PHILRACOM seeking stronger control over betting and racing: PHILRACOM sought a funding increase for racing operations and incentives. Its chairman described racing and breeding oversight, employment, revenue and possible sports tourism, while Senator Erwin Tulfo asked about stronger control over betting and racing. Authority over off-track betting and illegal-betting enforcement remained unresolved.
  • Answers often depended on later decisions: Agency officials described MTRCB compliance work, OMB inspections, FDCP grants and restoration plans, and PHILRACOM race review. Larger proposals involved digital powers, staff placement, FDCP funding and PHILRACOM off-track betting authority. DBM offered to coordinate on personnel placement as the transition planning continued.
