At a glance

  • Funding priorities and delivery: Senators sought passport-funding restoration, stronger tourism infrastructure, expanded school feeding and learning recovery, and support for science facilities. Classroom delivery, staffing and fund utilization were central to the education debate.
  • Household costs and employment: The Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas (BSP) discussed inflation, interest rates, investment and bank resilience. Other exchanges addressed travel taxes, export performance, worker transition and the effect of artificial intelligence on outsourcing jobs.
  • Public services and regulation: Members pressed for accessible overseas legal assistance, simpler public-service procedures and clearer information. Vaping exchanges questioned testing capacity, health-agency coordination, enforcement and youth protection.
  • Technology, culture and community rights: Members sought support for science scholarships, cultural exhibitions and creative industries. The Philippine Space Agency (PhilSA) defended domestic space capability, while the Bases Conversion and Development Authority (BCDA) faced questions about Pax Silica funding, affected families, consent and relocation.
