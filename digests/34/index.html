Official House of Representatives livestream

At a glance

  • Budget and service gaps: DILG presented a P332.5-billion proposal, with 84% for personnel services. Members questioned Remulla and officials of PNP, BFP, BJMP, NAPOLCOM, NYC, PCW, NCMF and ECCD about equipment, staffing and reductions affecting smaller agencies. Support for restoring or increasing funds was widespread, but proposed additions remained for further consideration.
  • School safety and police accountability: School shootings prompted proposals for safer gun storage, stronger firearm penalties, police and barangay support at schools, drills and restrictions on harmful online activity. Members disputed explanations centered on video games and pressed for mental-health safeguards. Separate exchanges challenged SONA arrests, Safer Cities apprehensions, drug lists and inadequate body cameras; officials defended operations and promised records or investigations, leaving disputed accounts unresolved.
  • Fire, rescue and jail capacity: BFP sought fuller access to accumulated Fire Code collections for trucks, stations, rescue equipment, ambulances and recruits. Later questioning clarified that the proposed Korean fire-truck purchase was still under discussion, with specifications and supplier selection pending. BJMP faced requests for more facilities, better records to prevent delayed releases and a review of restrictions on non-family visits.
  • Local funding and community oversight: LGSF discretion, duplication, disclosure and project completion drew repeated scrutiny. DILG distinguished its monitoring role from DBM releases and LGU implementation, cited engineers, local and third-party monitors and Subay Bayan, and promised reports. Members sought a fairer national-tax distribution, Local Government Code changes and higher SBDP support per barangay. CSO questions on outcomes, open data and grievance reporting were only partly answered.
  • Women, children and local services: Members pressed for stronger PCW regional reach, child development centers, functioning VAW desks, solo-parent benefits, youth mental-health and HIV programs, SK accountability and Hajj medical support. Officials cited local funding and staffing limits and sought additional resources. Remulla undertook adjustments to the PNP response to VAWC complaints, while several local infrastructure, personnel and enforcement concerns ended in promised follow-up.
  • Committee outcome: Suansing requested written additional-budget proposals in the first half of the following week for BARC consideration before plenary, and promised better handling of CSO questions. The committee approved termination of the FY2027 DILG and attached-agency hearing without objection, then suspended proceedings until September 1 at 9 a.m. The deliberation did not enact the proposed budget.

Agency presentation

P332.5-billion proposal and spending performance: Remulla introduced a P332.5-billion DILG proposal for 2027. The presentation described an increase of about 7%, with 84% for personnel services, 13% for maintenance and other operating expenses and 3% for capital outlay. It reported 2025 obligation and disbursement rates of 96% and 95%, respectively, and midyear 2026 rates of 55% and nearly 97%. BJMP, BFP, PNP and the Office of the Secretary would receive increases, while ECCD, PCW, NCMF, NYC and NAPOLCOM would receive less than their previous appropriations.
