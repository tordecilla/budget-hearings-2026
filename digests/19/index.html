At a glance

  • Who was questioned: Members chiefly questioned DOF on revenue, tax, debt, financing and privatization; DBM on appropriations, releases, safeguards and implementation; and DEPDev on growth, poverty, infrastructure and industrial strategy. BSP and other mandate-specific officials answered financial-sector and specialist questions.
  • Main fault lines: The hearing repeatedly tested whether the 2027 program could reconcile revenue and financing assumptions with poverty, employment, social-sector, infrastructure and regional-development priorities. Late questions focused on the lower 2026 revenue base, nominal-growth tax assumptions and privatization receipts.
  • Recurring official answers: Officials commonly grounded allocations in readiness, utilization, legal restrictions, fiscal space or replacement revenue, while distinguishing a requested policy from a commitment. DEPDev generally paired industrial or growth goals with structural requirements such as energy, connectivity and coordination.
  • Concrete submissions and follow-up: Several answers committed to submit or obtain information rather than provide it immediately, including GAD utilization data through PCW, accounts-payable detail, and education-sector and free-higher-education breakdowns or deficiency data.
  • Unresolved matters: The record leaves material questions for later resolution: detailed allocation and utilization data, the legal and booking treatment of earmarked funds, outstanding accounts-payable detail, the delivery of privatization receipts, and policy proposals for which officials offered only conditional consideration or study.
Reading note

Amounts, forecasts, legal interpretations, and policy positions are attributed to the speakers who gave them. A promised submission, study, or later coordination is not treated as an immediate answer or completed commitment.
