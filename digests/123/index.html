Official Senate video

At a glance

  • Opening agencies: The Office of the Vice President received parliamentary courtesy without questions. Commission on Audit exchanges covered its digitalization request and budget utilization; Commission on Human Rights exchanges covered abortion-policy positions and historical central-office construction.
  • Transportation and local development: Transport exchanges covered rail-funding restorations, remaining project cuts, delayed airport funding releases, fuel subsidies, and tourism access. Zamboanga City's special economic zone allocation was retained by the House and Senate committee.
  • Interior, housing, and environment: Interior discussions covered emergency response, tourist police, and domestic procurement. Housing additions and two Bukidnon projects were discussed. The environment sponsor sought stronger water-security funding; The Laguna Lake Development Authority was deferred while the other listed environment budgets were deemed submitted.
  • Sports and closing: Sports exchanges covered athlete allowances, grassroots access, facilities, and equal incentives. The sponsor promised plans and reports, while senators challenged the handling of suspected game-fixing and advance notice of drug tests.
