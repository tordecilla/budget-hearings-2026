At a glance

  • What the budget presentation covered: The Department of Transportation (DOTr) presented its proposed departmental allocation, project requests and funding included in the National Expenditure Program (NEP).
  • Project delivery and right of way: Senators focused on land acquisition for rail and airport work, including the subway, the North-South Commuter Railway, and Siargao. Officials described schedule and cost risks and committed to submit prioritized, bare-minimum funding needs.
  • Commuter service and access: The hearing addressed busway and service-contracting support, active transport, airport access and boarding bridges, regional rail service, port connections, and proposed motorcycle-taxi routes. Members asked agencies to explain service effects and identify priority projects.
  • Safety and public accountability: Discussion covered Coast Guard personnel and operating needs, airport screening, passenger safeguards, transport-related flooding, and community ports. Senators asked agencies to connect spending plans with reliable service, safety and measurable results.
  • Written follow-ups and plenary consideration: Officials agreed to provide prioritized project and right-of-way lists, a minimum Land Transportation Management System (LTMS) funding requirement, and outstanding project expenditure details. The committee submitted the budgets for plenary consideration subject to further discussion and written responses.

Department of Transportation (DOTr) budget

FY2027 proposed allocation and comparable request: For FY2027, the DOTr and its sectoral and attached agencies have ₱300.96 billion in the NEP, according to Assistant Secretary Cecilia E. Bien. She compared this with ₱197.3 billion in the FY2026 NEP and ₱137 billion in the separate FY2026 General Appropriations Act (GAA).
