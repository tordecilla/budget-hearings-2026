At a glance

  • FY 2027 COMELEC NEP and revised request: COMELEC Chairperson George Erwin M. Garcia warned that reduced election-preparation funding threatened the 2028 timetable. Proposed use of unspent BSKE funds remained subject to Congress and refinement of the special provision.
  • Election readiness and reforms: COMELEC Chairperson George Erwin M. Garcia described COMELEC's preparation priorities for the next national election and pointed to Bangsamoro practices — sector-based party-list classification, anti-political-dynasty rules, women's representation and candidate photographs — as possible national reforms requiring legislation. Members also asked about voter access, machine features and how COMELEC would preserve procurement flexibility while term-fixing legislation was pending.
  • Overseas and Bangsamoro voting: The hearing examined overseas internet voting, low registration and turnout, and COMELEC's plan for internet voting at 104 overseas posts. COMELEC Chairperson George Erwin M. Garcia also discussed the Bangsamoro parliamentary election, including voter information, local participation and electoral design; figures and progress described here are COMELEC's hearing statements.
  • Staffing and voter confidence: Members raised recruitment, vacancies, contract work and the pay of election officers, alongside concerns about voter registration and public confidence. COMELEC Chairperson George Erwin M. Garcia described unfilled posts and the agency's effort to fill some vacancies, while members urged that budget support and staffing keep pace with election operations.
  • What the committee did: Committee Chair Rep. Mikaela Angela B. Suansing brought DBM Director Elena Regina S. Brillantes into the discussion of the proposed BSKE-funds provision; Brillantes said DBM had no objection to the wording while leaving inclusion to Congress. COMELEC was asked to submit formal requests and reports. The committee approved a motion to terminate the COMELEC budget hearing and suspended proceedings until September 2, 2026.

COMELEC presentation

COMELEC's account of the 2025 election: FY 2027 COMELEC NEP proposal: PHP 29.5 billion, after an agency-described roughly PHP 15 billion reduction from a revised request. COMELEC Chairperson George Erwin M. Garcia described the 2025 national and local elections as successful and fast, citing Senate proclamations within five days, 81.7% turnout, and subsequent international recognition. He also reported three consecutive unqualified Commission on Audit opinions, a 99.73% financial-utilization rate, and a large reduction in the agency's pending case load. These are the Chairperson's reported performance measures.
