PCO presentation and connected questions

Mandate, communication programs and strategic priorities: Gomez described the PCO's mandate as coordinating clear, consistent government communication and informing the public about presidential and executive policies. Its work includes the Presidential Communications Program, Freedom of Information requests, press briefings, the Maging Mapanuri fact-checking workshops, coverage of presidential engagements, and coordination across agencies. He described attached-agency work in radio broadcasting, government communication materials, domestic and foreign information services, national printing, development communication through regional and provincial networks, and broadcast documentation of presidential activities.

He identified priorities including strategic government communications, media and information literacy, OPLAN Kontra Fake News, digital modernization, Bagong Pilipinas Studios, and energy conservation. He said the anti-fake-news effort included cooperation with nine national broadsheets and an anti-fake-news desk that refers reports to relevant agencies. His longer-term directions emphasized organizational agility, data-informed decisions, accountability, workforce development, presidential-event planning, media coverage, transparency, program communication, international representation, and state media.
