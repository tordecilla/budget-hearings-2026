Office of the President (OP): For fiscal year 2027, the Office of the President (OP) proposed National Expenditure Program (NEP) allocation was ₱10,155,758,000, inclusive of retirement and life insurance premiums; a separate original request to the Department of Budget and Management (DBM) was not stated. House Committee on Appropriations Senior Vice Chair Representative Albert S. Garcia presented it as about 0.14% of the ₱7.2 trillion national budget. By allotment class, the proposal comprised ₱7.46 billion for maintenance and other operating expenses (74%), ₱1.86 billion for personal services (18%), and ₱839.66 million for capital outlay (8%). By program, general administration and support received ₱2.74 billion and operations ₱7.41 billion; the four operations programs were Presidential Oversight (₱5.37 billion), Executive Staff Service (₱1.63 billion), Advisory (₱184.6 million) and Legal and Legislative Services (₱180 million). The special provision requires quarterly financial and physical reports within 30 days after each quarter, online posting, and notice to both houses and their appropriations and finance committees.

On the sponsor’s comparable basis, excluding retirement and life insurance premiums and pension, the FY2026 allocation of ₱28.03 billion fell to ₱10.08 billion in FY2027, a stated ₱17.95 billion (64.02%) decrease. Garcia attributed about ₱17.55 billion to completed FY2026 locally funded projects, principally Association of Southeast Asian Nations (ASEAN) 2026, and a further ₱392.52 million (3.75%) regular-program trim. The separate FY2027 NEP proposal should not be conflated with a distinct original agency request.

The confidential and intelligence funds (CIF) exchange addressed several scopes. Representative Antonio L. Tinio cited proposed FY2027 government-wide CIF of ₱10.77 billion and OP CIF of about ₱4.5 billion, or 45.22% of the OP FY2027 budget. Garcia described the aggregate under discussion as ₱11 billion plus, about 0.15% of the national budget; the exchange does not reconcile that description with Tinio’s separate agency and government-wide figures. Tinio cited reported releases of ₱500 million to Naga City (later described as more than ₱500 million), ₱500 million for a Lipa City multipurpose building and ₱30 million or less for most Financial Assistance to Local Government Units recipients; these were his distribution examples. Representative Renee Louise M. Co characterized Barangay Development Program support as ₱5 million per barangay while challenging its safeguards; Garcia promised documentation, without confirming a separate appropriation in that exchange. Representative Sarah Jane I. Elago asked whether the ₱39 million in FY2027 maintenance and other operating expenses for the Office of the Presidential Adviser for Marawi Rehabilitation and Development (OPA-MRD) covered consultation with displaced Marawi residents and requested a breakdown; Garcia gave the amount, and no breakdown was supplied in that exchange. A ₱125 billion FY2027 block grant for the Bangsamoro Autonomous Region in Muslim Mindanao (BARMM) was also discussed, outside the OP allocation.

Representative Edgar R. Erice, said the Office of the President’s proposed budget represented a 64% reduction from its 2026 allocation and considered a ₱4 billion intelligence and confidential fund justified in light of the West Philippine Sea situation.
