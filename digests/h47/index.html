At a glance

  • Opening remarks: House leaders framed civil-society participation across the budget cycle and described the consultation format before representatives took the floor.
  • Civil-society questions and proposals: Representatives raised sector and process concerns spanning agriculture and food security, education and social services, transport and road safety, local-fund safeguards, community priorities, disclosure, and earlier access to budget discussions.
  • Panel replies: House members explained review and amendment procedures, acknowledged missed or unclear engagement, and offered referrals, follow-up, or consideration of proposals. Expressions of support and proposed changes remained distinct from approved appropriations.
  • Afternoon participation framework: The presentation discussed a proposed Philippine Budgeting Code, local-to-national planning, outcome measures, public access to budget information, and ways to make civil-society participation more consistent.
  • Closing commitments: House members said they would circulate the draft Budget Code for CSO comments, accept questions for later stages, and strengthen performance information and channels for tracking public recommendations.
Reading note
