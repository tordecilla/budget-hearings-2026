At a glance

  • Budget and public priorities: Committee Chair Senator Joseph Victor "JV" G. Ejercito led deliberations on the FY2027 proposal for the Department of Economy, Planning, and Development (DEPDev) and its attached agencies. Secretary Arsenio M. Balisacan described the department's planning, investment coordination, and monitoring role. Senators tested how public spending could support recovery while preserving accountability for flood-control projects.
  • National ID and civil registration: The Philippine Statistics Authority (PSA), represented by Undersecretary Claire Dennis S. Mapa, addressed National ID costs and use, registration gaps, late birth registrations, second-ID requests, and physical-card printing. Mapa said authentication use was rising and offered reports; he also agreed to share blocked late-registration information. Senators sought stronger civil-registry data and safeguards, while the status of proposed legal changes remained pending.
  • Food prices, enforcement, and data: Senator Francis Kiko Pangilinan pressed Balisacan on rice prices despite imports and tariff changes. Balisacan cited possible market competition problems, logistics, and trade restrictions, while lacking current information about the onion-cartel penalty raised by Pangilinan. On differing PSA and Department of Agriculture estimates, officials confirmed an agreement for PSA data to govern official output statistics; Mapa promised a copy. Questions also covered agricultural-smuggling enforcement, pork-producer consultation, and El Nino preparedness.
  • Infrastructure and plenary follow-through: Senators raised regional planning, flood control, water retention, rail links, public-private partnership projects, energy security, and the timing of public investment. Balisacan supported long-term infrastructure planning and said prospective rail financing still required new preparation; he endorsed exploring an oil-storage proposal subject to fiscal and economic review. Ejercito said agencies should prepare answers and documents for plenary, then deemed the agency budgets submitted for plenary consideration subject to discussion and document submission; this was a procedural submission, not appropriation approval.

Agency budget summary

The hearing does not state a consolidated original request total submitted to the Department of Budget and Management (DBM), so that request-to-NEP comparison is unavailable.

Registration gaps and National ID use: Mapa said 97 million citizens had been registered against a 2026 population estimate of 114 million, leaving 17 million unregistered.
