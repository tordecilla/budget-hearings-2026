Official Senate video

At a glance

  • Purpose and scrutiny: Presiding Senator Risa Hontiveros framed social protection around timely help, appropriate targeting and measurable effects amid economic and climate pressures. Members pressed the Department of Social Welfare and Development (DSWD) on assistance, caseload changes, audit findings and the responsibilities shared with other agencies.
  • Programs and delivery: Questions covered Pantawid Pamilyang Pilipino Program (4Ps) targeting and graduation, crisis assistance, social pension, livelihood and disaster response, Tara, Basa! and Aral tutoring, water access, and verification of disability IDs. Answers described agency roles, implementation limits, data checks and service-delivery changes.
  • Civil-society and community priorities: Social Watch Philippines raised attached-agency restoration, child protection, disability support and accessible services. Samahan ng Nagkakaisang Pamilyang Pantawid (SNPP) described 4Ps inclusion and exclusion problems and paperwork burdens; the Coalition of Services of the Elderly (COSE) pressed for broader pension coverage and reliable beneficiary data.
  • Commitments and follow-up: Officials committed to provide program evaluation results, certification and audit or corrective-action reports, and to continue validating assistance records. Some questions about funding basis, beneficiary counts and agency accountability remained open in the hearing.
  • Plenary consideration: Hontiveros conditionally endorsed the FY 2027 budgets of DSWD and its attached and supervised agencies, including the National Anti-Poverty Commission (NAPC) and Presidential Commission for the Urban Poor (PCUP), for Senate plenary consideration subject to clarifications and commitments made during the hearing. This was a referral for consideration, not appropriation approval.
