  • Patient costs and service delivery: Health Secretary Edwin M. Mercado presented the Department of Health (DOH) program and answered questions with Philippine Health Insurance Corporation (PhilHealth) President and Chief Executive Officer Dr. Beverly Lorraine Ho; members tested whether appropriations and benefit design would reduce patient costs and improve access.
