Amendment procedure and outcomes

Scope and authority of the 2027 amendment review: Chair Rep. Mikaela Angela B. Suansing said the Budget Amendments Review Subcommittee (BARSC) was established to make budget review more transparent, broaden public participation and align amendments with urgent public needs. In its second consecutive year, the BARSC would deliberate on amendments to the proposed 2027 National Budget arising from agency requests. Only institutional amendments submitted by agencies and signed by the agency head would be considered. The BARSC would also take account of recommendations from Committee on Appropriations deliberations and the two weeks of Plenary deliberations. Its actions are recommendations: the House and Plenary retain authority to act on the proposed amendments and recommendations.
