At a glance

  • Budget, technology and staffing: Members focused on COA audit resources, information-technology modernization, participatory audit work and hard-to-fill auditor positions. They discussed additional funding needs and a proposed salary-standardization exemption and retirement measure.
  • Confidential-fund accountability: COA and senators examined current confidential-fund controls and proposed revisions, including alias documentation, disbursing officers, proof for information purchases and informer rewards, and protection of sensitive identities.
  • Fraud audits and flood-control oversight: The hearing covered fraud-audit referrals, notices of disallowance, audit findings on flood-control projects, regional and project-level accountability, and the need for coordinated master planning and technical review.
  • Public reporting and participation: Members asked about timely annual audit reports and public access, how civil-society partners are selected for participatory audits, and what documents COA would provide. The motion to submit the budget for plenary action passed subject to further discussion and requested documents.
