At a glance

  • Budget execution and fiscal space: Members questioned low or differently measured utilization, procurement delays, and whether DICT could deliver existing appropriations before seeking more. Agencies and legislators raised additional needs, but a budget-augmentation motion remained deferred when the committee voted to end the hearing.
  • Connectivity, reliability and affordability: Free Wi-Fi and broadband discussions focused on whether remote schools, barangay halls, islands and underserved areas had working service, not simply installed equipment. Members asked for site lists, local validation, restoration, affordable access and clearer measures of who could connect.
  • Online safety and enforcement: Members pressed for stronger responses to scams, impersonation, harmful content and risks to children. Officials described SIM and national-ID checks, platform coordination, referrals and law-enforcement work, while legislators continued to question implementation, accountability and safeguards.
  • Privacy, data location and sovereignty: The hearing debated how government and personal data should be protected, who remains responsible for records, and when information may be hosted outside the country. DICT described classification and localization planning; privacy officials and members emphasized lawful access, confidentiality and limits on personal-data sharing.
  • Digital-government results: Members distinguished downloads, verified accounts, API calls and listed services from active use and completed transactions. They sought service-continuity and end-to-end reporting as adoption grew, while officials described eGov services, cloud dependencies, cybersecurity work and plans to improve measurement.
  • Digital jobs and local capability: DICT described training and job-matching plans but said earlier employment outcomes were difficult to track. Legislators also raised public-sector IT staffing and retention, locally developed platforms, procurement choices and whether digital programs were delivering usable opportunities.
