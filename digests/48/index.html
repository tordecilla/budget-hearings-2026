At a glance

  • Budget and delivery: Members compared requests with proposed allocations and questioned low utilization, delayed releases, local-government liquidation and how funding would reach projects and services. Department of Transportation (DOTr) and Department of Budget and Management (DBM) described planned follow-up and release conditions.
  • Public transport and access: Questions covered modernization and driver support, active-transport facilities, rail operations and expansion, airport and port projects, and access for commuters and passengers. Members sought clearer implementation plans, performance information and local coordination.
  • Safety and accountability: The hearing addressed passenger and maritime safety, service reliability, agency oversight, incident response and requests for records or reports. Responses included explanations, commitments to provide information and matters left for further study or coordination.
  • Proceedings outcome: Members expressed support for the proposed DOTr budget. The committee then terminated the budget hearing and separately suspended its hearing on House Bill No. 10858 until September 7, 2026, at 9:00 a.m.; these actions did not approve an appropriation.
