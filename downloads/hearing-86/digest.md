## GCG presentation

### Mandate, governance results and reform priorities

The GCG described its mandate under Republic Act No. 10149, the GOCC Governance Act of 2011, as oversight of government-owned or -controlled corporations (GOCCs), including rationalizing the sector, ensuring qualified board appointments, evaluating performance and setting compensation and position-classification policies. As of June 30, 2026, it supervised 112 GOCCs across nine sectors. The sector’s average Corporate Governance Scorecard rating was 67.14%, above the 61% 2025 Agency Fiscal Targets; GCG said it assessed all GOCCs. Corpus corrected the approval date of the Compensation and Position Classification System II (CPCS II) to September 2025. No GOCC application for implementation had been received in the first half of 2026. For the 2025 talent pool, 38.25% were included in the shortlist against a 37% target. The sector’s performance-evaluation average was 75.70% against a 78% target, while all qualified GOCC submissions were subjected to performance target-setting, exceeding the stated 80% target. For 2026, scorecard validation was underway with an 80% target, dependent partly on supporting Commission on Audit (COA) and Anti-Red Tape Authority (ARTA) reports; 28 COA-audited reports and partial ARTA customer-satisfaction results had been received by June 30.
