## Presidential Communications Office budget

### PCO proper FY2027 NEP allocation and components

For the Presidential Communications Office (PCO) proper, Secretary David M. Gomez identified PHP 784 million in the FY2027 National Expenditure Program (NEP). He said it was about 19% below the FY2026 General Appropriations Act (GAA) level and 37.6% below the original FY2027 proposal; he did not state the exact original total in this passage. Gomez attributed a PHP 187 million net decrease to a PHP 71 million increase in personal services for 62 DBM-approved contractual positions, offset by reductions of PHP 235 million in maintenance and other operating expenses (MOOE) and PHP 23 million in capital outlay because proposed Tier 2 projects were not included. The FY2027 NEP mix was 63% MOOE and 37% personal services, with no capital-outlay allocation.
