## Philippine Reclamation Authority and PIEC presentation

### PIEC’s tourism and convention case

Lopez said the PIEC would be a 140,000-square-meter exhibition and convention venue intended to attract meetings, incentives, conventions and exhibitions (MICE), trade fairs, and association congresses that existing facilities could not accommodate. He contrasted the cited capacities of current venues with the PIEC’s scale and said the project could attract an additional 300,000 to 500,000 delegates at a 50–60% utilization rate. He also compared its prospects with Singapore’s trade expo, which he said had a 120,000-square-meter area and attracted 1.5 million participants.

On visitor spending, Lopez said a regular tourist spends P6,000 per night or day, while a business-event tourist brings in P30,000 because a corporation or government funds the visit and the tourist sets up booths. He argued that the PIEC could generate activity across tourism, commerce, real estate, transport, services, hotels, shopping, and restaurants, and act as an anchor development attracting hotel owners. He said PRA wanted to draw some MICE tourism from Singapore, which he cited as having 1.5 million MICE participants at Singapore Expo.
