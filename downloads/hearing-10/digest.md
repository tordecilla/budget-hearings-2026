## Agency presentation

### FY 2027 defense budget and strategic priorities

The FY 2027 DND NEP regular fund was PHP 324.632 billion. DND Secretary Gilberto C. Teodoro Jr. said the budget needed to support equipment with bases, logistics and trained personnel as defense technology and threats changed. He urged a flexible modernization system and described sovereignty and territorial integrity as a new mission area: DND had to maintain overwatch of a stated 2.4 million-square-kilometer maritime domain, protect Philippine jurisdiction over exploration and exploitation, and build strategic bases for the Pacific and Philippine Rise as well as the West Philippine Sea. Internal peace, a continued presence in islands and BARMM election-security support remained foundations for external defense.
