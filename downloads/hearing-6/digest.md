- **FY 2027 NEP and DOJ request:** Members pressed DOJ on reduced funds for protection work, prosecution staffing, systems and corrections. Officials identified proposed additions and members supported restorations; the hearing recorded no adopted appropriation.

- **Budget fault line:** Members sought restoration for DOJ operating funds, NBI and BI systems, cybercrime, anti-trafficking and child-protection work, legal staffing and corrections. Officials specified several proposed additions, but the hearing did not adopt these as appropriations.

- **Prosecution and accountability:** Questions distinguished DOJ's reported prosecution-success and disposition measures from conviction rates, exposed missing treatment of witness-related dismissals, and pressed for disaggregated graft, terrorism-financing, petition-for-review, and imprisonment data. Confidential-fund increases and liquidation were also scrutinized because the record did not reconcile all yearly components or explain the 2025 surge through public program outputs.

- **Protection and emerging threats:** The hearing linked reduced anti-trafficking, cybercrime, and OSAEC funding to AI-enabled grooming, sextortion, online recruitment, rescue capacity, international cooperation, and staff workload. DOJ sought at least prior-year funding for several protection lines, while questions about religious profiling, blacklist safeguards, gender-sensitive investigations, responder support, and technology-facilitated violence produced principles and review promises more often than measurable coverage or outcomes.
