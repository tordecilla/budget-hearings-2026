### FY2027 allotment mix and drivers

54% of the FY2027 proposed budget of the Department of Foreign Affairs (DFA) and its attached agencies was for maintenance and other operating expenses (MOOE), 39% for personnel services and 7% for capital outlays. DFA Assistant Secretary Emilio T. Fernandez attributed the changes to the following drivers. Personnel-service changes were attributed to the fourth tranche of the updated salary schedule and foreign service allowance increases, and to integrating the Technical Cooperation Council of the Philippines into DFA. MOOE changes included initial next-generation passport funding, foreign-exchange and inflation provisions for foreign service posts (FSPs), higher FSP rent contracts and MOOE for information systems strategic plan (ISSP) projects; capital outlay changes included ISSP investment. He said 2027 had no appropriation for opening additional FSPs.
