- **Budget execution and land-delivery capacity:** Members questioned why initial land-documentation targets rise while downstream survey and distribution targets fall, and raised the effect of budget ceilings on delivery. DAR officials cited funding and documentation constraints, described the pending release process for landowner compensation, and offered a supporting payment list.

- **Titles, conversion, and farmer protections:** Members raised beneficiaries not installed on land covered by Certificates of Land Ownership Award (CLOAs), overlapping claims, agricultural conversion, and farmer tenure after a court reversal in Sariaya. DAR described title and land-status review, coordination with other agencies, and protections for beneficiaries; members sought clearer records, safeguards, and a timeline for the Sariaya task-force report.

- **Farm bridges and foreign financing:** Members examined site selection and the farm bridge project. DAR described proposals originating with farmer organizations and validation with planning and local partners. The French loan remained unsigned, and members discussed how loan proceeds would require an appropriation before they could be spent.

- **Reporting, coordination, and close:** Members requested beneficiary, land-balance, conversion, compensation, and project data. Officials made several commitments to submit lists or reports and improve coordination. Gonzales moved to terminate the briefing; after a second and no objections, Dy approved the motion.
