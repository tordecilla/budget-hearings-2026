## Civil Service Commission presentation

### Workforce scale and CSC staffing

FY 2027 CSC proposal to DBM: PHP 4.8 billion; DBM-approved NEP ceiling: PHP 4.04 billion for the same commission. CSC Chairperson Marilyn B. Yap framed the proposed 2027 budget around CSC's national mandate. She reported a civil-service workforce of more than 2.25 million as of June 2026—2.02 million career and more than 232,770 non-career professionals—and more than one million job-order and contract-of-service workers, for a government workforce above 3.29 million. She said 73.6% of career professionals were in national government agencies, while 60.6% of non-career professionals and most JO/CO workers were in local government units. Against that scope, she reported CSC's 1,690-position allocation, 1,541 filled positions, 149 vacancies, and one CSC second-level-and-up employee for every 3,199 government workers.

### Seven-day solo-parent leave for JO and COS workers

Nazal asked why the seven-day solo-parent leave for qualified job-order (JO) and contract-of-service (COS) workers was not expressly included in the 2025 CSC-COA-DBM joint circular on ancillary benefits, citing Republic Act 11861 and its implementing rules. She said CSC had found a 2004 circular allowing seven days of solo-parent leave, had circulated it to departments, and was in dialogue with DSWD, which she identified as the Act's primary implementing agency, to clarify the entitlement. Nazal said departments remained reluctant to implement it because of audit or disallowance concerns. Yap said the joint circular already involved DBM and COA and addressed those concerns, but agreed that its relevant provision could be reiterated.

### Pathways from JO/COS work to vacant plantilla positions

Nazal asked for a government-wide plan, targets and timetable to match qualified JO/COS workers to unfilled plantilla positions, assist those without eligibility, and reduce reliance on contractual work; he said CSC's inventory counted 919,868 JO and COS workers.
