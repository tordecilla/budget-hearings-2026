### DOH budget consideration suspended

After about 29 majority members had expressed interest in questioning the Department of Health (DOH), Deputy Minority Leader Percival “Perci” Cendaña said he had been slated to ask questions in the minority’s second round and moved to suspend consideration of the DOH’s proposed budget, citing members’ wish to raise further issues with DOH officials.
