### FY2027 proposed allocation and comparable request

For FY2027, the DOTr and its sectoral and attached agencies have ₱300.96 billion in the NEP, according to Assistant Secretary Cecilia E. Bien. She compared this with ₱197.3 billion in the FY2026 NEP and ₱137 billion in the separate FY2026 General Appropriations Act (GAA).

Committee Chair Senator Joseph Victor "JV" G. Ejercito opened the FY2027 budget deliberation for the DOTr, stating that the NEP proposed ₱302.89 billion for the department. ### Rail progress, foreign-assisted funding and delay costs

Ejercito said he had inspected the North-South Commuter Railway (NSCR) and the Metro Manila Subway Project in Valenzuela with DOTr Secretary Giovanni Z.
