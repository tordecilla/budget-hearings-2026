- **Resources for public audit:** COA entered the FY 2027 briefing with no capital outlay in the NEP and a larger original proposal. Chairperson Gamaliel A. Cordoba and officials faced questions about auditor shortages; members sought stronger preventive audit and digital capacity.

- **Preventing and investigating irregularities:** Members pressed for pre-audit with geotagging, follow-through on flood-control findings and tighter confidential-fund rules. - **Cash balances versus cash access:** Rep. She cautioned that fiscal space would limit funding and did not promise a higher budget. The COA briefing was terminated and committee proceedings suspended; no final augmentation amount was approved in the closing motions.
