### Machines, precinct crowding and contingency capacity

Garcia said COMELEC leased rather than purchased voting machines for the 2025 election and attributed savings to avoiding warehousing, upgrades and maintenance. He said the proposed reduction would leave the agency short of machines for 2028, potentially forcing it back to 800 to 1,000 voters per precinct instead of its preferred 600, and sought capacity that included contingency machines.

### Why preparatory work must begin in 2027
