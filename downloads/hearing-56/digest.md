## At a glance

- **Budget requests and fiscal space:** The Department of Migrant Workers (DMW) and Overseas Workers Welfare Administration (OWWA) sought additional resources for overseas Filipino workers (OFWs). Presiding Senator Joel Villanueva heard DMW Secretary Hans Leo J. Cacdac, Undersecretary Dominique Rubia-Tutay and OWWA Administrator Patricia Yvonne "PY" M. Caunan. Cacdac said earlier utilization had affected the allocation despite later crisis needs. Members supported the requests, while Finance Committee Chair Senator Joseph Victor "JV" G. Ejercito stressed limited fiscal space and made no funding promise.

- **Emergency repatriation and delivery constraints:** Caunan said original emergency repatriation appropriations were fully used, while a supplemental released only in late July still had an available balance. Officials argued that contingency capacity must be maintained even when emergencies are unpredictable. OWWA also relied heavily on job-order staff, and an added personnel-services allocation lacked the corresponding released position items.

- **Assistance, reintegration and recruitment protection:** Cacdac described legal, medical and return assistance through the Action Fund, with blood money excluded and host-country medical insurance used first where available. Officials described training, job matching and livelihood pathways for workers and families, including those who might work abroad again. DMW warned about scammers impersonating licensed recruiters and promised to catch up on adjudication and appealed cases.

- **Access and service commitments:** Cacdac committed to relay OFWs' passport-fee concerns to the Department of Foreign Affairs (DFA), assign a Migrant Workers Office in Nagoya and pursue the OFW Hospital's planned Cancer Care Center. Proposed hospital wards would extend access beyond Pampanga. Villanueva submitted the FY2027 budget for plenary consideration, subject to the discussions and requested documents, then suspended the briefing.

## Department of Migrant Workers budget

### DMW expense classes, program allocations and operating requirements

Rubia-Tutay reported combined FY2027 DMW and OWWA expense classes of PHP4.026 billion for personnel services (34% of the budget), PHP7.513 billion for MOOE and PHP77 million for capital outlay. She described PHP1.837 billion for general administrative support (GAS) and PHP8.9 billion for operations. Against FY2026 GAA, she cited OSEC general administrative support down PHP88 million (9%) and operations down PHP105 million, attributing the reductions to lower budgets for the OFW Hospital, Migrant Workers Offices (MWOs) and National Maritime Polytechnic (NMP). She also said OSEC general administration and NMP budgets decreased by PHP90 million and PHP200 million, respectively, affecting continuing support services. 

The central office held 47% of the budget, where the centrally managed Action Fund and reintegration fund were lodged and then distributed to MWOs and regional offices. The presentation assigned 32% to MWOs, 12% to regional offices, 6% to the OFW Hospital and 3% to NMP. Rubia-Tutay said the 17 regional offices and 43 MWOs made up the operations group and received most operational funding, including Action Fund distributions.

For OSEC, the PHP2.810 billion personnel-services allocation covered salaries, allowances, benefits and premiums for field posts and terminal benefits for mandatory retirees. MOOE and capital outlay supported management and the Overseas Employment and Welfare Program, including MWO operations, employment placement and facilitation, the OFW Hospital, Action Fund and reintegration. The NEP also funded licensing and regulation, anti-illegal recruitment and trafficking work, labor migration policy and international cooperation. The NMP regular operating budget was PHP79.711 million, less the continuing Information Systems Strategic Plan (ISSP) budget of PHP16.265 million. Capital outlay included PHP3.5 million for protective equipment and PHP50 million for a training building. Regional-office operating budget (PSMOOE and capital outlay) was PHP824.24 million, including the Negros Island Region.
