### FY 2027 budget and anti-corruption mandate

The Office of the Ombudsman's FY 2027 NEP recommendation was PHP 7.232 billion, including automatic appropriations, against its PHP 12.796 billion original proposal. Ombudsman Jesus Crispin C. Remulla described the work as gathering facts and evidence and filing cases, a slow process that must proceed carefully through Regional Trial Courts and the Sandiganbayan.
