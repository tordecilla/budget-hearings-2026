## Agency presentation

### Anti-corruption mandate and FY 2027 recommended budget

The FY 2027 DBM-recommended NEP for the Office of the Ombudsman was PHP 7.232934 billion including automatic appropriations, or 0.1% of the stated PHP 7.2-trillion National Expenditure Program. Its constitutional anti-corruption mandate spans fact-finding and case build-up, prosecution, administrative adjudication, graft prevention and public assistance. The fiscal-autonomy framework in R.A. No. 6770 prevents a reduction below the preceding year's appropriation. The recommended allocation comprised PHP 3.760765 billion for personnel services, PHP 2.444135 billion for MOOE, PHP 857.1 million for capital outlay, PHP 168.332 million in automatic appropriations, and a PHP 2.602 million Military Camp Sales Fund share.
