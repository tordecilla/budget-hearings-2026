### Registration gaps and National ID use

Mapa said 97 million citizens had been registered against a 2026 population estimate of 114 million, leaving 17 million unregistered. He identified about 7 million of that gap as children aged 0-4, who were not yet required to register, and described overseas Filipinos as another large group; PSA had been unable to conduct registration visits in the Middle East amid security concerns but would resume when access allowed. Separately, on birth registration, Mapa said about 97% of the population had birth records, leaving roughly 3.5 million without them, including a substantial number in the Bangsamoro Autonomous Region in Muslim Mindanao (BARMM).

On use, Mapa said some agencies still use their own IDs, while social protection programs including 4Ps use the National ID database for biometric authentication and duplicate detection. He said the ID had been used for 114 million authentications from January through September 15, 2026, mostly in private financial services. PSA projected about 150 million authentications for the year, around 1.5 times registrants, and said use could grow to four or five authentications per registered person.
