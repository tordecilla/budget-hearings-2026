- **Budget and priorities:** DENR's FY 2027 proposal covered forest restoration, land patents, mineral development, water services and ecosystem protection. Members pressed DENR, MGB, EMB, BMB, LWUA, NWRB and LLDA on local delivery and funding gaps.

- **Flooding and water:** Members called for coordinated river-basin planning, stronger watershed and mangrove funding, hazard-informed infrastructure and assessment of Bulacan Airport's effects on Pampanga. Suansing requested a formal, prioritized DENR submission for the Budget Amendments Review Subcommittee. ## Agency presentation

### P28.04-billion proposal and natural-capital priorities

The FY 2027 DENR NEP allocation was PHP 28.04 billion for DENR, its 17 regional offices, bureaus and attached agencies, against an original PHP 55.040579 billion DBM request including Tier 2. With a mandate covering 15.8 million hectares of classified forestlands and over 36,000 kilometers of coastline, DENR Acting Secretary Juan Miguel T. Cuna framed the priorities around community impact despite limited fiscal space. He proposed shifting the National Greening Program toward forest-landscape restoration and community enterprises, issuing up to 43,000 land patents in 2027, and pursuing a 2% mining contribution to GDP by 2028 through small-scale mining support, critical-mineral mapping and regulatory enforcement.

### Water, waste and ecosystem resilience

Cuna proposed P124 million in assistance to local water districts for isolated and unserved barangays. He reported that extended producer responsibility had mobilized over 1,000 enterprises and diverted 244 million kilograms of packaging waste, alongside continued work on Manila Bay, the Pasig River and Laguna Lake. Cuna reported an operating 24-hour geohazards center, interventions targeting 18 high-exposure provinces, and stronger management of 121 legislated protected areas and marine networks. DENR was building technical capacity and staffing to establish site-specific ecosystem accounts under PENCAS for planning and budgets.
