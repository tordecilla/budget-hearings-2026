## At a glance

- **Economic outlook and budget choices:** Senators pressed the sponsor and economic managers on weaker growth, inflation, exchange-rate pressure, debt and fiscal space. A recurring fault line was whether spending would produce near-term household relief and productive investment while maintaining fiscal discipline.

- **Project delivery and accountability:** Members questioned infrastructure reductions, delayed awards, project readiness, incomplete documentation and safeguards after the flood-control controversy. The sponsor described tighter review and early procurement, while senators sought clearer implementation information and stronger follow-through.

- **Households and productive services:** The discussion linked the budget to health coverage and out-of-pocket costs, school meals and scholarships, food security and farmers, targeted assistance, electricity costs, jobs and transport. Senators also raised preparation for El Nino and how funding would reach vulnerable communities.

- **Commitments and open questions:** Officials committed to budget documents and direct briefings, a plan for Philippine Health Insurance Corporation (PhilHealth) arrears, review of the electric-cooperative revolving fund, and public-works progress updates. DBM would provide a representative for later plenary debates, and the Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas (BSP) Governor was required to return the next day. Infrastructure restorations, new-law funding, debt-payment detail and immediate household relief remained open for follow-up or amendments.

- **Proceedings:** The Senate closed interpellation and debate on the general principles. The DBM, DEPDev and DOF budget presentations followed later in the session; each was deemed submitted after no interpellators came forward.
