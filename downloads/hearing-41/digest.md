## Department of Education (DepEd) budget

### DepEd appropriation components, program funding and utilization

DepEd proper's personnel services represented about 79% of its budget, approximately PHP 766 billion. Compared with the fiscal year 2026 General Appropriations Act (GAA), maintenance and other operating expenses (MOOE) declined by PHP 33 billion and capital outlay by PHP 67 billion.

The FY2027 NEP highlights included PHP 12.09 billion for expanded career progression targeting more than 135,000 teachers and school heads; PHP 3.75 billion for training more than 500,000 teachers and laptops for 50,000 teachers; PHP 17.31 billion for more than 4,000 classrooms; PHP 3.37 billion for internet renewal in more than 14,000 schools, information and communications technology (ICT) solutions and capacity building; PHP 6.4 billion for textbooks, workbooks and other learning materials; PHP 2.63 billion for inclusive education; and PHP 11.43 billion for the feeding program serving 3.69 million learners. DepEd also sought additional support for inclusive education, governance and innovation without stating an amount. For FY2026, DepEd reported obligation utilization of 51.54% and disbursement of 89.28% as of August 20, 2026.
