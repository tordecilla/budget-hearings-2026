## PAGCOR presentation

### Income performance, gaming decline, and the 2027 outlook

PAGCOR presented PHP 106.03 billion in total income for 2025, an estimated PHP 86.95 billion for 2026, and projected 2027 income of PHP 88.33 billion with PHP 5.45 billion in net income. It reported PHP 95.14 billion in 2025 gaming-operations income, down PHP 2.38 billion from 2024, and attributed the decline to the POGO closure, weaker PAGCOR-casino and licensee revenue, and the delinking of e-wallets from online gaming platforms. The 2026 and 2027 amounts were estimates and projections, not reported final results.

## Rep. Mikaela Angela B. Suansing

### Lower gaming income and PAGCOR's recovery outlook

Suansing asked why the stated 2026 total-income projection of about PHP 86.95 billion was 18 percent below the PHP 106-billion 2025 actual income. PAGCOR Chairman Alejandro Tengco attributed the decline to the delinking of e-wallet payment applications from online gaming, which he said was followed by about a 40-percent fall in gaming activity, and to the Middle East crisis affecting land-based and online markets.
