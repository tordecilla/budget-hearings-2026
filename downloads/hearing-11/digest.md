## At a glance

- **FY2027 NEP and requests:** DHSUD and the shelter agencies sought funding for housing programs and project completion beyond the FY 2027 NEP. Members supported increases, while DBM cited corporate income, prior allotments and incomplete utilization; no increase was approved.

- **Housing people can use:** Questions centered on access for low-income households, the gap between completed inventory and loan takeout, vacant or flood-prone relocation sites, missing amenities and livelihoods, and water-service arrears. Officials described expanded rental, transitional and community mortgage options alongside 4PH financing.

- **Disaster assistance and unresolved cases:** Members challenged exhausted funds and delayed releases for fire and disaster victims. Officials described prospective releases, while requests on estate-management funding, legal help for private land disputes and local dispute resolution remained unanswered or lacked a mechanism.

- **Commitments to follow:** DHSUD undertook to provide proclamation status updates and an employee housing survey, coordinate with evicted families, and use remaining interest-subsidy and ISSP funds before year-end. Committee Chair Rep. Mikaela Angela B. Suansing requested ranked budget amendments within the week, including options for partial funding.

- **Outcome:** The briefing was terminated after Deputy Speaker Jose C. Alvarez moved and Committee Chair Rep. Mikaela Angela B. Suansing seconded. The closing motions did not approve the budget or the requested increases; committee proceedings were then suspended for resumption on August 26 at 9 a.m.

## Agency presentation

### FY 2027 housing budget and response to housing need

The FY 2027 NEP allocated PHP 5.498 billion to DHSUD and key shelter agencies against an approximately PHP 60 billion aggregate original DBM submission. Secretary Jose Ramon P. Aliling said the PSA's current estimate is 3.7 million housing needs by 2028, rather than the previously cited 6.9 million. DHSUD's proposed response combines the Community Mortgage Program (CMP), expanded SHFC support for repairs, site development and utilities, Pag-IBIG home-improvement loans, 4PH, and private-sector production.
