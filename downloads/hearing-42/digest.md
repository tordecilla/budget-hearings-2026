- **Staffing and fiscal autonomy:** Members pressed for the organization of newly created courts and the filling of judicial vacancies. Officials explained the staffing-action process and discussed implementing fiscal autonomy, with requests for court and staffing details to follow.

- **Access and protection:** Members asked how legal aid reaches indigent and remote communities and sought beneficiary and regional data. - **Augmentation submissions and hearing close:** Members voiced support for the Judiciary budget, including requests for measurable performance and accountability. ### Budget composition, capital projects and additional requests

For the Supreme Court and lower courts, the presenter described personnel-spending growth, a PHP 3.246 billion increase in Maintenance and Other Operating Expenses (MOOE), and a PHP 3.758 billion capital-outlay decrease. She explained that the 2026 GAA included an allocation for the Manila Hall of Justice, while the 2027 proposal included PHP 200 million for the Cebu and Cagayan de Oro Judiciary Complexes. She then described further project needs: PHP 500 million for Cagayan de Oro and PHP 4.541 billion for Cebu, PHP 5.041 billion in total, and said she hoped these would be carried in the 2027 GAA. For PET, she cited increases of PHP 11.239 million in personal services and PHP 2.320 million in MOOE. For the Court of Appeals, she cited increases of PHP 522.731 million in personal services, PHP 849.167 million in MOOE and PHP 140.890 million in capital outlay.
