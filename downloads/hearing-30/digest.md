## At a glance

- **Proposed investments:** Department of Transportation (DOTr)'s proposed investments covered rail, road, aviation and maritime projects, but members questioned which projects were funded, partly funded or left without proposed allocations.

- **Release and utilization:** Members and officials discussed how foreign-loan proceeds, counterpart funding, right-of-way acquisition and unprogrammed appropriations affect project release, utilization, delays and financing costs.

- **Commuter programs:** Members sought stronger commuter support and challenged proposed cuts to EDSA Bus Carousel service contracting and fuel subsidies; DOTr supported restoration, with no committee funding decision in that exchange.

- **Regional projects and promised reports:** Regional requests included rail connectivity, right-of-way, ports, roads and airport improvements. Some remained unfunded or conditional on inspection, while agencies promised project lists, timelines, delay reports and alignment documents.

- **Budget augmentation proposal:** A proposed lump-sum augmentation for unfunded projects and studies remained pending without a vote; other increase requests and expressions of support also remained distinct from adopted appropriations.

- **Maritime safety and suspension:** Members questioned Maritime Industry Authority (MARINA) on inspection staffing and technical-capacity needs. The hearing then recessed for lunch; the recess did not approve or conclude the budget deliberation.

### DOTr 2027 NEP allocation and budget composition

For fiscal year 2027, the DOTr NEP allocation was ₱300.96 billion, as presented by DOTr Assistant Secretary Cecilia E. Bien. That was ₱103 billion (52.52%) above its FY2026 NEP figure of ₱197.3 billion; against the FY2026 GAA amount of ₱137.846 billion, the FY2027 NEP was ₱163.114 billion (118%) higher. These are separate comparisons using different FY2026 bases. DOTr said ₱243 billion, or 81.05% of its FY2027 NEP, was capital outlay, while personnel services received ₱35.35 billion (11.75%). The Office of the Secretary, including Land Transportation Office (LTO), Land Transportation Franchising and Regulatory Board (LTFRB), MRT-3, CAR and CARAGA, was reported at 83.71% or ₱251 billion; the Philippine Coast Guard (PCG) was separately reported at ₱45.551 billion (15.12%). These figures are stated as components of the allocation; the transcript total in that breakdown is garbled. The presentation did not give a distinct total for DOTr's original agency-wide submission to Department of Budget and Management (DBM); the later ₱624 billion figure is specifically its proposed projects, not an established total agency request. For the Office of the Secretary, Central Office alone, FY2025 obligation and disbursement rates were 90.45% and 57.45%; as of July 2026 they were 82.40% and 51.73%. Across DOTr and attached agencies, FY2025 rates were 89.35% and 56.94%, and as of July 2026 they were 74.10% and 56.18%.
