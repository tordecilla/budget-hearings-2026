### Public information, media literacy and digital production

PCO's program combined presidential coverage and coordinated agency messaging with freedom of information, fact-checking workshops, regional and provincial communication, public radio, and government print and audiovisual production. Its priorities included media literacy, an anti-fake-news reporting desk with referrals to appropriate agencies, digital platforms and audience analytics, and additional Bagong Pilipinas Studios. Gomez also proposed energy-saving equipment and transport measures. He framed these activities around simpler internal processes, decisions informed by data, measurable accountability, and stronger staff capabilities.
